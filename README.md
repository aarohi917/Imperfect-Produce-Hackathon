# Imperfect Produce 

Imperfect Produce is a farm-management application for farmers and organic vegetable businesses, with an initial focus on mainland China. It connects harvest records, Checker approvals, agricultural and financial analysis, field mapping, and an AI farmer's guide in separate farmer and management workspaces.

## Purpose

The app helps farmers organize their daily work and helps higher management keep track of multiple farms, produce batches, and recorded incoming and outgoing movements (imports/exports). Connecting these records with review and notifications makes it easier to understand what was harvested, where produce is going, and which information has been checked.

Tracking food across organizations and borders is a recognized international challenge: FAO describes effective traceability as an ongoing challenge across complex global supply chains. [2](https://www.fao.org/food-safety/food-control-systems/traceability---recalls/en) Imperfect Produce addresses part of this problem through organized farm records and accountable review; it is not currently a customs, international shipping, or end-to-end supply-chain tracking system.

Its long-term aim is to support better-informed farming decisions, make safe imperfect vegetables more commercially useful, and reduce avoidable waste. These are intended outcomes, not yet proven income or waste-reduction results.

## Main Features

- **Role-specific workspaces:** Farmers see operational information, while authorized Checkers and CEOs access management tools and financial information.
- **Harvest records and approvals:** Record crops, kilograms, grades, fields, dates, photos, and destinations; harvest inputs and outputs require Checker approval.
- **Agricultural and financial insights:** Compare approved harvests by crop, field, grade, and destination. Financial analysis uses recorded costs and expected revenue, not assumed sales or payments.
- **Field Intelligence:** Organize farms and zones, import supported geographic boundaries, assign responsibilities, report field issues, and review supporting evidence.
- **AI farmer's guide:** Connect advice to accessible farm records and curated research, with saved conversations, confirmed private memory, goals, and optional mood check-ins.
- **Monitoring and weekly updates:** Summarize approved records and highlight traceable issues and goal progress, subject to role permissions and configured delivery.
- **Visual Intelligence:** Use camera-based produce detection and estimated quality observations, then send an annotated photo report to both Checkers and CEOs for review.
- **Phone access and localization:** An installable web app with Chinese, English, Hindi, and Marathi, plus manually selected currency and time-zone settings.
